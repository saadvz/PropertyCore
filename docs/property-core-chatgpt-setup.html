Property Core · ChatGPT guide

Set up Property Core in ChatGPT

Property Core runs one of two helpers on your phone number. Tour Core lets people text to book a self-guided tour of your rental. Cleaner Core texts your cleaner about each turnover and hands over the door code once the guests have left.

You don't need any technical skills. Pick the one you're setting up, then follow the steps in order.

About 20 minutes5 steps5 stepsNeeds a computer (not the phone app)

Which are you setting up?

Your number runs one at a time. You can switch later by asking ChatGPT.

Stuck at any point? That's normal. Two easy ways out:

1. In your ChatGPT chat, type "I'm stuck on step 2" (or whichever step) and describe what you see. ChatGPT will walk you through it.

2. Or text Saad the step number and what the screen says. A screenshot helps, as long as it doesn't show a door code or a secret code.

Before you start

Have these ready. If one is missing, a step will stop partway through.

Your own details

You type these during setup

Two phone numbers

Your mobile number and your cleaner's. With both, you get a group chat with your cleaner. Only have one number? Enter it as the cleaner's number too, and you get a chat with the assistant instead.

Your own details

Property basics at hand

Your property's address, usual checkout and check-in times, your cleaner's name, and your door code.

A few words you'll see

Here's what they mean. You don't need to understand more than this.

Sounds technical, but it's just a switch that lets you add Property Core. You turn it on once.
Property Core
The add-on that connects ChatGPT to Tour Core or Cleaner Core. You add it once.
Scheduled task
A helper ChatGPT makes for itself. It checks for news once an hour.
Private link
A one-time web page where you type anything secret. Safer than the chat.
Practice tour
A pretend tour run to check everything works. Nobody gets a text.
Publish
Turning tours on, so visitors can book by text.

The steps for Tour CoreCleaner Core

Tick "Done" as you finish each step. The ticks are remembered in this browser, so you can take a break and come back.

1

Turn on Developer Mode

ChatGPT settings
  1. On your computer, go to chatgpt.com and sign in.
  2. Click your name or picture in the corner, then Settings.
  3. Click Apps & Connectors (on some accounts just Connectors).
  4. Click Advanced, and switch Developer Mode on.

2

Add Property Core to ChatGPT

ChatGPT settings
  1. Still in Apps & Connectors, click the button to add a new one. If it asks what kind, pick custom MCP server.
  2. In Name, type Property Core.
  3. In the box that asks for a URL (a web address), paste this:
    https://tourcore-production-8b80.up.railway.app/mcp
  4. For Authentication, pick OAuth. Leave everything else. Click Create.
  5. A page titled Connect ChatGPT to Tour Core and Cleaner Core opens. Type the invite code Saad sent you.
  6. Under What do you want to set up?, pick Tour Core: self-guided tours for a rental unit.
  7. Click Allow.

Only for Cleaner Core, and only if your bookings are on a Google calendar: connect Google Calendar in the same list.

Add Property Core once. The same connection runs Tour Core and Cleaner Core.

3

Answer ChatGPT's questions

Chat with ChatGPT
  1. Start a new chat. Click + next to where you type and add Property Core. ChatGPT can only use it once it's added.
  2. Type Set up Tour Core and press Enter.
  3. ChatGPT asks one question at a time. When it shows buttons, pick one. Otherwise answer in your own words. It will ask about:
    1. The street address, and then checks it with you.
    2. The kind of place: a single-family home, a multifamily home, or one apartment or condo.
    3. Which places people can tour, and what to call them (like Unit A, or the street name).
    4. How someone walks in: the door they come in through, then the way to the door they tour.
    5. A phone number stuck visitors can call during tour hours. You can skip this.
    6. What days and times people can tour. Different days can have different hours.
    7. Questions visitors often ask: how to apply, fees, lease lengths, what's due at move-in, bringing someone, building rules, the area. Skip any you like.
    8. Whether visitors fill out a short identity form first. It's recommended.

ChatGPT asks you to approve something? If it matches what you just asked for, click approve.

Not sure how to answer? Just say so. "I don't know, what do most people pick?" works fine.

4

Turn on tour updates

Chat with ChatGPTScheduled tasks
  1. ChatGPT asks "Want me to tell you when someone books, starts, or finishes a tour, and when something needs you?" Say yes.
  2. ChatGPT makes a scheduled task called Tour Core by itself. It checks for news once an hour. You don't copy anything.
  3. ChatGPT asks "Want tour updates texted to your phone too?" Pick Urgent only (recommended) and give your mobile number, so a visitor waiting for a time reaches you right away.

The texts never include a visitor's name. They just tell you to open your chat.

5

Check it and go live

Chat with ChatGPT
  1. ChatGPT reads your setup back as a short list. If something's wrong, just say what to change.
  2. ChatGPT runs a readiness check and a practice tour. Nobody gets a text and no real door opens.
  3. If something needs fixing, ChatGPT tells you in plain words and fixes it with you.
  4. When both pass, ChatGPT asks "Everything passed. Want me to publish this for a demo?" Say yes.
  5. ChatGPT tells you the number visitors text to book a tour. Share that number in your listing.

You're done! 🎉 That's the whole setup.

What happens from now on

Visitors text your number, and Tour Core handles the booking and the tour.

Most days you don't need to do anything. You hear when something needs you.

Visitors

People text to book

Someone texts your number, picks a time, and fills out the identity form if you turned it on.

Tour time

Tour Core guides them

It texts them the way in and checks in near the end of the tour. You don't have to be there.

Every hour

ChatGPT checks in

Its task posts what's new. Urgent things also come to your phone if you picked Urgent only.

Any time

Ask ChatGPT

Try "what tours are booked today?", "pause tours", or "change the tour hours".

Group text

Your cleaner texts

You see their message right away in the group text. ChatGPT picks it up at its next hourly check. To reply, tell ChatGPT "Maria texted, draft a reply".

Door code

The code waits for your yes

Your cleaner only gets the door code after you've said the guests have left.

Summary

Cleaning finished

Your assistant looks at the cleaner's photos, checks them against your checklist, and sends you a short report.

If something goes wrong

Click a problem to see what to do.

It says my invite code "isn't right", "has expired", or "was already used"

Each invite code works only once, and only for 7 days.

What to do: text Saad for a new code, then connect again.

ChatGPT set up the wrong one (tours instead of cleanings, or the other way)

The other choice was picked on the approval page.

What to do: tell ChatGPT "switch to Tour Core" or "switch to Cleaner Core". If nothing is live yet, it switches right away.

ChatGPT says to start a new chat, or can't find the right tools

New tools only show up in a fresh chat.

What to do: start a new chat and say "Set up Tour Core" or "Set up Cleaner Core". Your answers so far are saved.

The practice tour didn't pass

Something needs fixing, like a missing door or tour hours.

What to do: ChatGPT says what's wrong. Answer its question, and it checks again.

Setup is finished but my cleaner never got a text

Usually the next booking's times aren't confirmed yet.

What to do: tell ChatGPT "confirm the times for the next booking". If it says nothing was sent, text Saad.

"This tool call was blocked by OpenAI's safety checks"

Usually a false alarm.

What to do: type "try again".

ChatGPT asks what Property Core is, or gives a general answer

What to do: click + and add Property Core to the chat. Do this in every new chat.

ChatGPT warns it hasn't checked in, or a task is paused

What to do: turn the task back on from the scheduled tasks page.

Anything else

What to do: type "I'm stuck" in the chat and describe what you see, or text Saad the step number and what the screen says.

When you ask Saad or anyone else for help, never send a door code, your invite code, or any secret code from setup.
