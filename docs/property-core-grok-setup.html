Property Core · Grok guide

Set up Property Core in Grok

Property Core runs your rental's jobs from one chat, property by property. Cleaner Core texts your cleaner about each turnover and hands over the door code once the guests have left. Supply Core keeps the place stocked: you pick each product once, the bot shops, and every cart is checked before checkout.

You don't need any technical skills. Pick a flow to see its steps, then follow them in order.

Which flow are you setting up?

A property can run both. Set up one now, and turn the other on later by asking the bot.

Coming soon: Fixer Core, for repairs. Self-guided tours (Tour Core) are on hold for new hosts, and Saad sets them up by hand. Their guide is here.

Stuck at any point? That's normal. Two easy ways out:

1. In your bot's chat, type "I'm stuck on step 2" (or whichever step) and describe what you see. The bot will walk you through it.

2. Or text Saad the step number and what the screen says. A screenshot helps, as long as it doesn't show a door code, a card, or a secret code.

Before you start

Have these ready. If one is missing, a step will stop partway through.

Your Grok account

Access to Grok Bots

You need a Grok account that can add bots. If you're not sure yours can, ask Saad.

Your phone, or Saad

A way to sign in

Where sign-up is open, you create an account with a code texted to your phone. Otherwise Saad sends an invite code. It only works once, and only for 7 days.

You type these during setup

Two phone numbers

Your mobile number and your cleaner's. With both, you get a group chat with your cleaner. Only have one number? Enter it as the cleaner's number too, and you get a chat with the assistant instead.

Your own details

Property basics at hand

Your property's address, usual checkout and check-in times, your cleaner's name, and your door code.

Grok's Vault

A way to pay

A card, Shop Pay, Link, or X Money saved in Grok's Vault. The bot pays from there. Supply Core never sees your card.

Your own details

Your shopping list

The property's address and everything you want restocked, up to 30 items. Store logins help too: the bot can save them in your Vault with you.

A few words you'll see

Here's what they mean. You don't need to understand more than this.

Bot
Your own copy of Grok, set up to run your property. It has its own chat.
Flow
One job the bot runs at a property: Cleaner Core or Supply Core. A property can run both.
Choice card
Buttons the bot shows for a question. Tap one to answer.
Secure card
A special box in the chat for anything secret, like a door code. What you type there stays private.
Routines
Little helpers the bot makes for itself, so it keeps working while you're away.
Vault
Where Grok keeps your cards and store logins. They never go to Supply Core.
Held cart
A cart Supply Core stopped before checkout, like a price jump or a cart over your cap. Nothing is bought until you say yes.

3

Answer the bot's questions

Bot chat
  1. The bot asks one question at a time. When it shows buttons, pick one. Otherwise answer in your own words. It will ask about:
    1. Your property: its name, address, and usual checkout and check-in times.
    2. Where your bookings are (Guesty, Google Calendar, Airbnb and so on).
    3. Who's involved: your mobile number and your cleaner's number. Two numbers set up a group chat with your cleaner. If you only have one number, give it as the cleaner's number too, and you'll get a chat with the assistant.
    4. Timing: how early to offer each cleaning, when to remind, and hours not to text.
    5. Your door system, then your door code.
    6. Your cleaning checklist and anything else you prefer.
  2. For your door code (and a calendar link, if it asks), the bot shows a secure card in the chat. Type it in the card and submit it, not as a normal message.
  3. If your bookings are on a Google calendar, the bot may ask you to connect Google Calendar. Follow its link and sign in.

Not sure how to answer? Just say so. "I don't know, what do most people pick?" works fine.

Connect the alerts

RoutineBot chat

After your last answer, the bot makes its two routines by itself. One needs two codes from you, so the bot knows when your cleaner texts.

  1. The bot sends two links, each with a secure card under it: first the Webhook URL, then the Webhook key.
  2. Click the first link, copy what it shows, come back, and paste it in the first secure card. Do the same with the second.
  3. The bot posts "Cleaner Core alerts are connected." That means it works.

Use the two links the bot sends. Opening the Routines page yourself won't show the Webhook URL.

Turn it on

Bot chat
  1. The bot shows a summary of everything you told it. If it all looks right, type Activate. If something's wrong, say what to change first.

  3. Your cleaner gets one text asking them to reply YES. You might let them know it's coming.

Answer the bot's questions

Bot chat
  1. The bot asks one question at a time. It will ask about:
    1. Your list: everything you want restocked, up to 30 items. It reads the list back once.
    2. Where to buy: stores that deliver to the door, like Walmart, Target, or Costco. Maybe also a same-day service, like Instacart, for rush orders.
    3. Schedules: one for everything, or one per item. Every so many days, a day of the month, or once every so many stays.
    4. A product for each item. Tap your pick from the product cards. Then a backup if you want one, and how many to buy each time.
    5. Your booking calendar, only if an item goes by stays and none is saved yet. Use the secure card for the link.
    6. Hands-off ordering, or stop at checkout for your yes.
    7. Spending caps: per order, per month, both, or none.
    8. The check time for the daily check. 10:00 unless you pick another.

Not sure how to answer? Just say so. "I don't know, what do most people pick?" works fine.

The daily check and your stores

RoutineBot chat
  1. The bot makes a routine called Supply Core Check by itself. It runs every day at your check time. You don't copy anything.
  2. It checks each store, without ordering: it's signed in (a login saved in your Vault, or the store's connector), the delivery address is your property's, and a payment method is ready.
  3. If something's missing, the bot tells you and helps you add it to your Vault.

Never type a password or card number in the chat. Cards and logins go in your Vault.

What happens from now on

Most days you don't need to do anything. You hear when something needs you.

Bot chat

Your cleaner texts back

The bot posts a short summary right away. Reply to it in plain words, like "yes, the guests have left".

Door code

The code waits for your yes

Your cleaner only gets the door code after you've said the guests have left.

Summary

Cleaning finished

Your assistant looks at the cleaner's photos, checks them against your checklist, and sends you a short report.

Every day

The bot checks what's due

At your check time, Supply Core Check finds what's due and shops for it. With nothing to do, it posts nothing.

Checkout

Every cart is checked

Before any order, Supply Core checks the final cart: the items, the total, and the delivery date. If it passes, the bot orders and tells you. A held cart waits for your yes: buy anyway, use the backup, or skip.

Your Vault

Your cards stay yours

Card details never reach Supply Core. Grok pays from your Vault. If Grok shows its own pay card, tap Pay.

Any time

Running low?

Say "we're low on paper towels". With Cleaner Core too, your cleaner's "running low" answer after a cleaning turns into an order.

If something goes wrong

Click a problem to see what to do.

It says my invite code "isn't right", "has expired", or "was already used"

Each invite code works only once, and only for 7 days.

What to do: text Saad for a new code, then connect again.

I picked the wrong flow, or want to add the other one

You can change it any time.

What to do: tell the bot "turn on Supply Core" or "turn off Cleaner Core". If anything is live, it shows what stops and waits for your yes. Your setup is kept.

Grok says to start a new chat, or can't find the right tools

New tools only show up in a fresh chat.

What to do: start a new chat and say "Set up Property Core". Your answers so far are saved.

Setup is finished but my cleaner never got a text

Usually the next booking's times aren't confirmed yet.

What to do: tell the bot "confirm the times for the next booking". If it says nothing was sent, text Saad.

I can't find the Webhook URL

It only shows from the link the bot sent. What to do: type "Send me the webhook links again".

The bot won't activate Supply Core

Every store in your plan has to be ready first: signed in, delivering to the property, with a way to pay.

What to do: ask the bot "which store isn't ready?" and add what's missing to your Vault with it.

The bot says a cart is held

Something changed, like a price jump or a cart over your cap. Nothing was bought.

What to do: answer in the chat: buy anyway, switch to the backup, or skip.

The bot link doesn't open, or there's no "Add to Grok Bot" button

What to do: sign in to Grok and open the link again. Still no button? Open a new Grok Bot and paste this message instead. Then go on from step 2, after its first line.
