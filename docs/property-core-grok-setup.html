Property Core · Grok guide

Set up Property Core in Grok

Property Core runs one of two helpers on your phone number. Tour Core lets people text to book a self-guided tour of your rental. Cleaner Core texts your cleaner about each turnover and hands over the door code once the guests have left.

You don't need any technical skills. Pick the one you're setting up, then follow the steps in order.

About 20 minutes4 steps5 stepsA ready-made bot

Which are you setting up?

Your number runs one at a time. You can switch later by asking the bot.

Stuck at any point? That's normal. Two easy ways out:

1. In your bot's chat, type "I'm stuck on step 2" (or whichever step) and describe what you see. The bot will walk you through it.

2. Or text Saad the step number and what the screen says. A screenshot helps, as long as it doesn't show a door code or a secret code.

Your property's address, usual checkout and check-in times, your cleaner's name, and your door code.

3

Answer the bot's questions

Bot chat
  1. The bot asks one question at a time. When it shows buttons, pick one. Otherwise answer in your own words. It will ask about:
    1. Maybe: whether to keep a private copy of your records in a folder you own. Skipping is fine.
    2. The street address, and then checks it with you.
    3. The kind of place: a single-family home, a multifamily home, or one apartment or condo.
    4. Which places people can tour, and what to call them (like Unit A, or the street name).
    5. How someone walks in: the door they come in through, then the way to the door they tour.
    6. A phone number stuck visitors can call during tour hours. You can skip this.
    7. What days and times people can tour. Different days can have different hours.
    8. Questions visitors often ask: how to apply, fees, lease lengths, what's due at move-in, bringing someone, building rules, the area. Skip any you like.
    9. Whether visitors fill out a short identity form first. It's recommended.
  2. The bot asks "Want me to tell you when someone books, starts, or finishes a tour, and when something needs you?" Say yes. The bot sets up the updates by itself. You don't copy anything.
  3. It asks "Want tour updates texted to your phone too?" Most people pick No texts, since the bot already posts right away.

Not sure how to answer? Just say so. "I don't know, what do most people pick?" works fine.

Check it and go live

Bot chat
  1. The bot reads your setup back as a short list. If something's wrong, just say what to change.
  2. The bot runs a readiness check and a practice tour. Nobody gets a text and no real door opens.
  3. If something needs fixing, The bot tells you in plain words and fixes it with you.

Answer the bot's questions

Bot chat
  1. The bot asks one question at a time. When it shows buttons, pick one. Otherwise answer in your own words. It will ask about:
    1. Your property: its name, address, and usual checkout and check-in times.
    2. Where your bookings are (Guesty, Google Calendar, Airbnb and so on).
    3. Who's involved: your mobile number and your cleaner's number. Two numbers set up a group chat with your cleaner. If you only have one number, give it as the cleaner's number too, and you'll get a chat with the assistant.
    4. Timing: how early to offer each cleaning, when to remind, and hours not to text.
    5. Your lock, then your door code.
    6. Your cleaning checklist and anything else you prefer.
  2. For your door code (and a calendar link, if it asks), the bot shows a secure card in the chat. Type it in the card and submit it, not as a normal message.
  3. If your bookings are on a Google calendar, the bot may ask you to connect Google Calendar. Follow its link and sign in.

Not sure how to answer? Just say so. "I don't know, what do most people pick?" works fine.

What happens from now on

Visitors text your number, and Tour Core handles the booking and the tour.

Most days you don't need to do anything. You hear when something needs you.

Visitors

People text to book

Someone texts your number, picks a time, and fills out the identity form if you turned it on.

Tour time

Tour Core guides them

It texts them the way in and checks in near the end of the tour. You don't have to be there.

Bot chat

The bot tells you

When a tour is booked or something needs you, the bot posts right away. Reply to it in plain words.

Any time

Ask the bot

Try "what tours are booked today?", "pause tours", or "change the tour hours".

Bot chat

Your cleaner texts back

The bot posts a short summary right away. Reply to it in plain words, like "yes, the guests have left".

Door code

The code waits for your yes

Your cleaner only gets the door code after you've said the guests have left.

Summary

Cleaning finished

Your assistant looks at the cleaner's photos, checks them against your checklist, and sends you a short report.

If something goes wrong

Click a problem to see what to do.

It says my invite code "isn't right", "has expired", or "was already used"

Each invite code works only once, and only for 7 days.

What to do: text Saad for a new code, then connect again.

Grok set up the wrong one (tours instead of cleanings, or the other way)

The other choice was picked on the approval page.

What to do: tell the bot "switch to Tour Core" or "switch to Cleaner Core". If nothing is live yet, it switches right away.

Grok says to start a new chat, or can't find the right tools

New tools only show up in a fresh chat.

What to do: start a new chat and say "Set up Tour Core" or "Set up Cleaner Core". Your answers so far are saved.

The practice tour didn't pass

Something needs fixing, like a missing door or tour hours.

What to do: the bot says what's wrong. Answer its question, and it checks again.

Setup is finished but my cleaner never got a text

Usually the next booking's times aren't confirmed yet.

What to do: tell the bot "confirm the times for the next booking". If it says nothing was sent, text Saad.

I can't find the Webhook URL

It only shows from the link the bot sent. What to do: type "Send me the webhook links again".

The bot link doesn't open, or there's no "Add to Grok Bot" button

What to do: sign in to Grok and open the link again. If the button still isn't there, text Saad.

Anything else

What to do: type "I'm stuck" in the chat and describe what you see, or text Saad the step number and what the screen says.

When you ask Saad or anyone else for help, never send a door code, your invite code, or any secret code from setup.
